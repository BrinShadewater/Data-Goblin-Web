## Why this licence

The whole point of this book is that the argument should be checkable and the
receipts should travel. A copyright default of "all rights reserved" would
have made it free to read and illegal to teach, which defeats the purpose.
NonCommercial keeps a print edition on the table without taking anything away
from the people this was written for.
